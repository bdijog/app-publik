## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a clinical, authoritative, and deeply trustworthy public digital gateway for the Balai Laboratorium Kesehatan Masyarakat (Labkesmas) Ambon under the Ministry of Health of the Republic of Indonesia (Kemenkes RI).

### Target Audience & Persona
The portal caters to healthcare institutions, clinics, regional hospitals (RSUD/RSUP), public health centers (Puskesmas), academic researchers, commercial businesses (food, beverage, hospitality), and private citizens requiring accredited testing across Maluku and Eastern Indonesia.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Authoritative & Certified**: Communicates national competence, precision instrumentation, and strict adherence to ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory quality management systems.
- **Sterile & Methodical**: Evokes cleanliness, scientific accuracy, and procedural certainty without feeling cold or bureaucratic.
- **Accessible & Transparent**: Offers straightforward fee structures, self-serve sample registration, clear sample delivery guidelines, and real-time step-by-step test tracking.
- **Confidential & Secure**: Instills ironclad confidence regarding test validity, personal health information privacy, and verified digital signatures.

### Design Movement: Institutional Precision Minimalism
The visual execution relies on editorial clarity paired with modern healthcare utility. It fuses an authoritative, institutional serif typeface (`Newsreader`) for high-level declarations and certification headers with the precision and structural legibility of `Manrope` for data density, parameters, and form layouts. Surfaces are crisp and restrained, relying on controlled depth, soft rounding, and high text contrast rather than heavy ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by deep institutional teal `#134E4A`, conveying environmental health expertise and governmental credibility, complemented by deep slate `#0F172A` for high-contrast typographic rendering.

### Primary Role
- **Deep Teal (`#134E4A`)**: Used for primary action buttons, credential headers, dominant visual markers, active stage milestones, and top-tier navigation anchor elements.
- **Teal Hover (`#0F3F3C`)**: Deeper variant for focused, active, and pressed interactions on primary controls.
- **Teal Surface (`#F0FDFA`)**: Ultra-light tint for table row selections, focused input containers, informational callouts, and selected filter pills.

### Secondary & Neutral Roles
- **Slate Body (`#0F172A`)**: Primary text color ensuring maximum contrast against light surfaces (AA/AAA compliance). Also deployed as the anchor background for high-level regulatory footers.
- **Slate Subtle (`#64748B`)**: Secondary metadata, SLA indicators, helper labels, and auxiliary descriptions.
- **Surface Background (`#F8FAFC`)**: Page backdrop for comfortable prolonged reading and data entry.
- **Card Background (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white elevated modules to frame discrete clinical information blocks.

### Functional Roles
- **Verified Green (`#16A34A`) & Tint (`#DCFCE7`)**: Official validation states, digital signature stamps, and published result badges.
- **Sky Progress (`#0284C7`) & Tint (`#E0F2FE`)**: Active laboratory testing indicators, physical sample delivery tracks, and fluid water analysis badges.
- **Pending Amber (`#D97706`) & Tint (`#FEF3C7`)**: Payment validation required, administrative queue holds, and sample resubmission notices.
- **Rejection Red (`#DC2626`) & Tint (`#FEE2E2`)**: Sample temperature non-compliance, volume insufficiency, or security mismatch errors.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs `Newsreader` and `Manrope` to reflect governmental authority alongside scientific exactness.

### Typographic Roles
- **Headlines (`Newsreader`)**: Conveys institutional heritage, ministerial dignity, and legal authenticity across all hero displays, section banners, certificate metadata headers, and verification outcomes.
- **Body & Data (`Manrope`)**: A contemporary humanist geometric sans designed for high legibility in complex forms, laboratory testing parameter tables, sample volume specs, and step-by-step tracker instructions.
- **Reference & Identifiers (`code-tracking`)**: Used for official FPPS numbers (e.g., `FPPS-2026-AMB-0142`), barcode annotations, and verification hashes with wide tracking for zero error tolerance during physical receipt comparisons.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a structured 12-column responsive grid engineered for dense technical catalogs and distraction-free transactional wizards.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adapters
- **Mobile (`< 640px`)**: Single column layout, 16px edge margins, 16px row gaps. The FPPS tracking stepper collapses into a vertical timeline with connecting spine lines.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`)**: 6-column grid with 24px gutters. The 17 testing catalogs adapt into a two-column bento system with compact sticky parameter calculators.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px - 1440px`)**: Standard 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of 1280px, flanked by 32px to 48px outer margins. Supports 3-column service catalogs and dual-panel tracking views (Status & Timeline on left; Sample Metadata & Verification Details on right).

### Spacing Philosophy
Consistent multiples of 4px and 8px govern all layouts:
- `space-xs` (4px): Gap between icon and accompanying metric badge text.
- `space-sm` (8px): Form label to input field separation; badge padding.
- `space-md` (16px): Card internal compact padding; field-to-field vertical gap.
- `space-lg` (24px): Standard card container internal padding and section module spacing.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section-to-section breaks and landing page sequence dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a crisp, sterile clinical aesthetic, depth relies primarily on tonal surfaces and hairline boundaries rather than dramatic shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Background color (`#F8FAFC`). Pure flat surface providing uniform high-contrast support.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Framing / Resting Cards)**: Surface color (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid hairline border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active / Hover / Dropdown)**: Applied during cursor hover on interactive test cards and open dropdown pickers: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(19, 78, 74, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. The border shifts to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navbars, Bottom Cost Bars & Modals)**: Applied to the sticky registration cost calculator and verification modals: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. A subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) is integrated with high-opacity backing (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces a soft, disciplined corner radius token system anchored at level `1` (`0.25rem` / `4px` base), producing a clean, clinical silhouette.

### Radius Application Guidelines
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 4px)**: Input fields, checkboxes, data table cells, and status indicators.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges (`rounded-lg`, 8px)**: Primary action triggers, category chips, and parameter tags.
- **Content Panels & Containers (`rounded-xl`, 12px)**: Main bento cards, sample upload dropzones, verification result summaries, and wizard step panels.
- **Full Radius (`rounded-full`)**: Timeline step counter bullets, circular accreditation seals, and floating action button avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep Teal (`#134E4A`) fill, crisp white Manrope 600 text, 8px radius, 12px vertical by 24px horizontal padding. Hover shifts smoothly to `#0F3F3C`.
- **Secondary / Outlined**: 1px solid `#CBD5E1` border, slate `#0F172A` text, white background. Hover transitions to `#F8FAFC` background with `#134E4A` border.
- **Danger Action**: Red (`#DC2626`) fill or ghost border for canceling active submissions.

### Category Chips & Parameter Pills
- **Neutral State**: Slate-50 background (`#F8FAFC`), 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`), 6px vertical by 14px horizontal padding, Manrope 600 (12px).
- **Active Selection**: Deep Teal surface (`#F0FDFA`), 1px solid border (`#134E4A`), Deep Teal text (`#134E4A`) accompanied by a leading checkmark icon.

### Form Inputs & Textareas
- **Default**: Pure white surface, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, 10px vertical by 14px horizontal padding, 4px radius. Manrope 400 (14px).
- **Focus**: Border shifts to `#134E4A` with an ambient focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(19, 78, 74, 0.15)`).
- **Helper & Error States**: Contextual text positioned 4px beneath field; red `#DC2626` for phone/FPPS format mismatches, slate `#64748B` for character counter limits.

### Sample Tracking Stepper
- **Completed Nodes**: Solid green `#16A34A` circle containing a checkmark, connected by a solid teal `#134E4A` rule line.
- **In-Progress Node**: Deep teal `#134E4A` ring pulsing softly, containing a high-contrast dot, paired with an active timestamp and lab unit notation.
- **Pending Nodes**: Muted slate `#CBD5E1` outline with subtle gray center dot and dashed connecting spine.

### LHU Certification Card & Verified Stamp
- **Structure**: High-contrast white card bordered by `#134E4A` hairline stroke with a light `#F0FDFA` header band.
- **Digital Seal**: Integrated circular emblem with Kemenkes RI / ISO 17025 accreditation marks, encrypted QR block, and dynamic green authenticity badge reading "DOKUMEN RESMI TERVERIFIKASI".

### Sticky Bottom Cost Estimator
- **Fixed Position**: Anchors to viewport bottom with 12px blur backdrop and Level 3 shadow elevation.
- **Summary**: Left aligned count of selected test parameters and calculated turnaround SLA; right aligned total cost formatted in Indonesian Rupiah with a prominent "Lanjutkan ke Pendaftaran" button.
